### Exploit analyze

This exploit enters a website index.php?mode=cirectory i taking off the list, all torents, add on the website, with them ID
Taking off one torrent (the last one from list) and constructs URL to form, upload screanshot for specific torrent upload_file.php?mode=upload&id=...
Sending fabricated request POST, in witch, name of it is test.php, but in header declares Content-Type image/jpeg. Contents that image, in reality is PHP code.
