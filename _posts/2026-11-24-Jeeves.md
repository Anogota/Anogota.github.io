This privilege allows a process to assume the identity of a different user, enabling it to perform actions or access recources as if it were that user.
However, if not properly managed or granted to unauthorized user or process, the SeImpersonatePrivilege can pose a significant security risk. The SeImpersonatePrivilege vulnerability can be exploited by malicious actors to conduct various attacks and gain unauthorized access on a system.
